35 Frequently Used Prepositions

Rules & Exercises

What are Prepositions?
Words that show relationships between nouns/pronouns and other words in sentences
Indicate time, place, direction, manner, and other relationships

Example: The book is on the table. (shows location)

The 35 Most Common Prepositions

  • about • above • across • after • against
  • along • among • around • at • before
  • behind • below • beneath • beside • between
  • beyond • by • despite • down • during
  • except • for • from • in • inside
  • into • near • of • off • on
  • out • outside • over • through • with

Prepositions of Time – Rules

AT – Specific times

Clock times: at 5 o'clock, at noon, at midnight
Special times: at Christmas, at the weekend
Example: I'll meet you at 3 PM.

ON – Days & dates

Days: on Monday, on Christmas Day
Dates: on July 4th, on my birthday
Example: The party is on Friday.

IN – Longer periods

Months: in January
Years: in 2023
Seasons: in summer
Example: I was born in December.

Prepositions of Place – Rules

AT – Specific points

at the door, at the bus stop, at school
Example: She's at the entrance.

ON – Surfaces

on the table, on the wall, on the floor
Example: The book is on the desk.

IN – Enclosed spaces

in the room, in the box, in the car
Example: The keys are in my pocket.

Direction & Movement Prepositions

TO – Movement toward

go to school, walk to the park
Example: She's going to the store.

INTO – Movement inside

walk into the room, jump into the pool
Example: He came into the house.

FROM – Origin/Source

come from work, a gift from friend
Example: I received a letter from my cousin.

THROUGH – Passing

walk through the park, look through window
Example: We drove through the tunnel.

Position & Relationship Prepositions

BETWEEN – Two items

between the chairs, between you and me
Example: The ball is between the two boxes.

AMONG – Three or more

among the crowd, among friends
Example: She felt comfortable among her classmates.

BESIDE – Next to

beside the table, beside me
Example: He sat beside his friend.

BEHIND – At the back

behind the door, behind the house
Example: The garden is behind the house.

Common Usage Prepositions

FOR – Purpose/Duration

for you, for a week, for studying
Example: This gift is for you.

WITH – Accompaniment

with friends, with a pen
Example: I went shopping with my mother.

BY – Agent/Means

by the author, by car, by 5 PM
Example: The book was written by Smith.

ABOUT – Concerning

about history, about you
Example: We talked about the project.

Advanced Usage Tips

Distinguishing Similar Prepositions

IN vs INTO: "in" = location, "into" = movement
She's in the room. vs. She walked into the room.

ON vs OVER: "on" = contact, "over" = above
The book is on the table. vs. The lamp is over the table.

BETWEEN vs AMONG: "between" = two, "among" = three+
The treaty between two countries. vs. She felt comfortable among friends.

Review & Key Points

Remember These Rules:

  1. AT = specific points (time/place)
  2. ON = surfaces & days
  3. IN = enclosed spaces & longer periods
  4. TO = movement/direction
  5. FOR = purpose/duration
  6. WITH = accompaniment
  7. OF = possession/relationship
  8. FROM = origin/source
