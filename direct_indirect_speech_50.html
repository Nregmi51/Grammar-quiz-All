1. StatementsSaid that + tense backshift. Pronoun change: I → he/she. If universal truth, no tense change.
2. QuestionsYes/No: if/whether + assertive order. Wh-: keep wh-word + assertive order. No question mark in indirect.
3. Commands / RequestsUse told / ordered / requested / advised + to-infinitive. Negative: not to + V1. Let's → suggested / proposed.
4. Exclamations & TruthsExclaimed with joy/sorrow/surprise + that. Universal truth & habitual facts keep same tense.

Section A: Statements (1-15)

Direct → Indirect
How to change: He said, "I am busy." → Remove comma & quotes, use that, change pronoun and tense: He said that he was busy. If reporting verb is past, present → past, will → would.

Section B: Questions (16-25)

Wh- / Yes-No
How to change: "Are you happy?" → He asked if/whether + sub + verb: He asked if I was happy. "Where do you live?" → Keep wh-word: She asked where I lived. No do/does/did in indirect.

Section C: Commands, Requests, Advice (26-35)

Imperative
How to change: "Please help me." → He requested me to help him. "Shut the door." → He ordered to shut the door / He told me to shut the door. "Let us go" → He suggested going / that they should go.

Section D: Exclamatory & Universal Truths (36-45)

Feelings & Facts
How to change: "What a beautiful flower!" → She exclaimed with joy that it was a very beautiful flower. Universal truth like "The sun rises in the east." → Tense does NOT change: Teacher said that the sun rises in the east.

Section E: Mixed SEE Style (46-50)

Exam Pattern
How to change: SEE often mixes since/yesterday/tomorrow. Remember: yesterday → previous day, tomorrow → next day, this → that, here → there, now → then.
