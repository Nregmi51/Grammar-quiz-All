Frequently Used Prepositions

Rules, Examples & Practice

A comprehensive guide to mastering prepositions in English

📚 What are Prepositions?

  • Words showing relationships between nouns/pronouns and other words
  • Indicate: time, place, direction, manner, or reason

🎯 Why Learn Them?

  • Essential for clear communication (e.g., "in the box" vs. "on the box")
"I am good in math" ❌
"I am good at math" ✅

Preposition "IN"

📦 Rules

  • Enclosed spaces: in a room, in a car
  • Time periods: in 2023, in the morning
  • Cities/countries: in Paris, in Canada

💡 Examples

  • The keys are in the drawer
  • She was born in 1995

✏️ Practice

"The cat is sleeping ___ the basket."

Answer: in

Preposition "ON"

📚 Rules

  • Surfaces: on the table, on the wall
  • Specific days/dates: on Monday, on July 4th
  • Transport: on the bus, on a plane (not in)

💡 Examples

  • The book is on the desk
  • We meet on Fridays

✏️ Practice

"The painting is hanging ___ the wall."

Answer: on

Preposition "AT"

📍 Rules

  • Specific points: at the door, at the bus stop
  • Precise times: at 5 PM, at noon
  • Addresses: at 123 Main Street

💡 Examples

  • I'll see you at the cinema
  • Class starts at 9 AM

✏️ Practice

"The party starts ___ 8 PM."

Answer: at

Preposition "TO"

➡️ Rules

  • Direction/Movement: go to school, run to the store
  • Recipient: give it to me, send a letter to Mom
  • Comparison: prefer tea to coffee

💡 Examples

  • We drove to the beach
  • This gift is to you

✏️ Practice

"Please pass the salt ___ me."

Answer: to

Preposition "FOR"

🎯 Rules

  • Purpose: for dinner, for fun
  • Duration: for 2 hours, for a week
  • Benefit: a gift for you

💡 Examples

  • I bought flowers for my mom
  • He studied for the exam

✏️ Practice

"We waited ___ an hour."

Answer: for

Preposition "WITH"

👥 Rules

  • Accompaniment: go with friends, with my family
  • Tools/Instruments: write with a pen, cut with a knife
  • Possession: the girl with red hair

💡 Examples

  • She danced with her partner
  • I write with a pencil

✏️ Practice

"He built the house ___ his hands."

Answer: with

Preposition "BY"

✍️ Rules

  • Agent (passive voice): written by Shakespeare
  • Transport: travel by car, by train
  • Deadline: finish by Friday

💡 Examples

  • The song was sung by Taylor Swift
  • We traveled by train

✏️ Practice

"Submit your essay ___ Monday."

Answer: by

Preposition "FROM"

🌍 Rules

  • Origin/Source: from Paris, from 9 to 5
  • Separation: take from the shelf
  • Material: made from wood

💡 Examples

  • She is from Japan
  • Butter comes from milk

✏️ Practice

"The train arrives ___ Boston."

Answer: from
