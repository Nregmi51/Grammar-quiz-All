Q.9 Reproduction

6×1 = 6
Set 1
a. She seldom visits the library, ________? tag
✓ does she?
b. By tomorrow, they ________ (complete) the bridge. verb
✓ will have completed
c. The teacher said to me, “Why are you absent today?” indirect
✓ The teacher asked me why I was absent that day.
d. The workers repaired the road. passive
✓ The road was repaired by the workers.
e. Mina likes spicy food. negative
✓ Mina does not like spicy food.
f. Raju will meet his uncle at the airport. 'Where' Q
✓ Where will Raju meet his uncle?
Set 2
a. Let us go for a walk, ________? tag
✓ shall we?
b. Look! The children ________ (play) in the garden. tense
✓ are playing
c. “Please help me with this box,” the old man said to the boy. indirect
✓ The old man requested the boy to help him with that box.
d. Did the police catch the thief? passive
✓ Was the thief caught by the police?
e. Everyone enjoyed the programme. negative
✓ Nobody disliked the programme.
f. She has lived in Nepal for ten years. 'How long' Q
✓ How long has she lived in Nepal?
Set 3
a. Nobody called you, ________? tag
✓ did they?
b. If I were rich, I ________ (help) poor children. verb
✓ would help
c. Sita said, “I have finished my assignment.” indirect
✓ Sita said that she had finished her assignment.
d. They are planting trees around the school. passive
✓ Trees are being planted around the school by them.
e. He always tells the truth. negative
✓ He never tells a lie.
f. The students went to Pokhara by bus. 'How' Q
✓ How did the students go to Pokhara?
Set 4
a. You hardly know him, ________? tag
✓ do you?
b. When I reached the station, the train ________ (leave). tense
✓ had left
c. The principal said to the students, “Do not make noise.” indirect
✓ The principal ordered the students not to make noise.
d. Someone has stolen my bicycle. passive
✓ My bicycle has been stolen.
e. She can solve the problem. negative
✓ She cannot solve the problem.
f. They are organizing a cultural programme. 'What' Q
✓ What are they organizing?
Set 5
a. I am late, ________? tag
✓ aren’t I?
b. By next month, he ________ (work) here for five years. verb
✓ will have been working
c. Hari said to me, “Will you attend the meeting?” indirect
✓ Hari asked me whether I would attend the meeting.
d. The students should obey the school rules. passive
✓ The school rules should be obeyed by the students.
e. Everybody likes the new teacher. negative
✓ Nobody dislikes the new teacher.
f. The woman bought a beautiful shawl from Kathmandu. 'What' Q
✓ What did the woman buy from Kathmandu?
